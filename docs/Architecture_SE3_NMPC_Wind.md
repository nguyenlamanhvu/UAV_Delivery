# Full Control Architecture: SE(3), NMPC & Wind Model

The UAV delivery system utilizes a hierarchical, cascaded control structure consisting of a high-level NMPC trajectory planner, a low-level SE(3) geometric controller, and an interactive Dryden Wind system within the Drake multibody physics engine.

---

## 1. Non-Linear Model Predictive Control (NMPC)
The NMPC module runs at a lower frequency to compute optimal time-horizon trajectories. It solves a constrained non-linear optimization problem over a receding horizon $N$.

### State and Input
* State $x = [p, v, R, \omega]^T$ (Position, Velocity, Rotation, Angular Velocity).
* Input $u = [T_1, T_2, T_3, T_4]^T$ (Individual rotor thrusts).

### Cost Function
The NMPC minimizes the following quadratic cost function over horizon $N$:

$$ J = \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \left( (x_k - x_{ref,k})^T Q (x_k - x_{ref,k}) + (u_k - u_{ref,k})^T R (u_k - u_{ref,k}) \right) + (x_N - x_{ref,N})^T P (x_N - x_{ref,N}) $$

Subject to constraints:
* **System Dynamics:** $x_{k+1} = f(x_k, u_k)$
* **Actuator Limits:** $0 \le u_k \le U_{max}$

The resulting optimized state targets $(p_{ref}, v_{ref}, \dot{v}_{ref})$ are pushed over LCM to the geometric controller.

---

## 2. SE(3) Geometric Controller with Adaptive Gains
The SE(3) controller executes high-frequency rigid body pose tracking using the targets provided by the NMPC planner. It tracks the pose seamlessly in the special Euclidean group $SE(3)$, avoiding Gimbal lock and enabling aggressive maneuvering.

### Translational Error
The position and velocity errors:
$$ e_p = p - p_{ref} $$
$$ e_v = v - v_{ref} $$

### Desired Thrust Vector
The total desired force vector $F_{des}$ incorporates feedforward acceleration, PD tracking, gravity compensation, and the estimated aerodynamic drag:
$$ F_{des} = -K_p e_p - K_d e_v + m (g e_3 + \ddot{p}_{ref}) - F_{drag,est} $$
Where the thrust magnitude is computed by projecting $F_{des}$ onto the drone's current vertical Z-axis ($R e_3$):
$$ f = F_{des} \cdot (R e_3) $$

### Rotational Error
The desired orientation $R_{des}$ is constructed to align the drone's Z-axis ($e_3$) with the normalized force vector $F_{des} / \|F_{des}\|$, while the X-axis is chosen to satisfy the commanded yaw.
The rotational error vector $e_R$ is defined using the skew-symmetric unmapping map $^\vee$:
$$ e_R = \frac{1}{2} (R_{des}^T R - R^T R_{des})^\vee $$
The angular velocity error $e_\Omega$:
$$ e_\Omega = \Omega - R^T R_{des} \Omega_{des} $$

### Torque Command
The required torque $\tau$ is:
$$ \tau = -K_R e_R - K_\Omega e_\Omega + \Omega \times J \Omega $$

The final inputs are mapped to the four individual propeller RPMs through the motor allocation matrix.

---

## 3. Dryden Wind Model & Drag System
The simulation engine injects aerodynamic forces onto the quadrotor's body using a Dryden Wind gust model combined with viscous drag.

### Wind Velocity
The wind velocity $v_{wind}$ at any time consists of a nominal steady wind vector and a stochastic turbulent gust $\Delta v_{gust}$ generated by a band-limited white noise filter:
$$ v_{wind}(t) = v_{nominal} + \Delta v_{gust}(t) $$

### Aerodynamic Drag Force
The relative velocity of the drone through the air mass is:
$$ v_{rel} = v_{drone} - v_{wind} $$

The drag force opposing the drone's motion is proportional to the square of the relative velocity and the drag coefficient $C_d$:
$$ F_{drag} = -\frac{1}{2} \rho C_d A \|v_{rel}\| v_{rel} $$

This external force acts on the `MultibodyPlant` physics simulator, challenging the SE(3) controller to reject the disturbance in real time.
